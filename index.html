PakkaKit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · Applies to the PakkaKit Android app (package com.ankitdev.tools)

In short: PakkaKit does not have accounts or servers. Your PDFs, photos, scans, calculator inputs and documents are processed and stored on your phone. We do not receive them. Google's on-device scanning and text-recognition components may send limited diagnostic information to Google (see section 4).

1. Who we are

PakkaKit is an independent app made by an individual developer, Ankit. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any government body or department. Contact: pakkakits@gmail.com

2. What the app does with your files

PakkaKit offers tools such as PDF merge, split and compress, photo tools, a document scanner, letter and invoice templates, measurement sheets and calculators. All of this work happens on your device.

  • You choose files with Android's own file picker. The app does not ask for access to all files on your phone.
  • Results are saved in the app's own storage on your phone and appear in the Files tab. You can delete them there at any time.
  • A file leaves the app only when you tap Share or Save and choose where it goes (for example WhatsApp, email or your Files app). That choice is yours, and the receiving app's own privacy policy applies from that point.
  • We do not upload your files, scans, photos or documents to any server of ours, and we do not have one.

3. Information stored on your phone

To save you typing, the app keeps some things on your phone only: your settings (theme, language), saved templates, saved addresses and party details, business details you enter (which can include GST or PAN numbers if you choose to type them), measurement sheets and a list of recent files.

  • This information is not sent to us.
  • If Android backup is switched on in your phone settings, Android may back up the app's saved settings and templates to your own Google account and restore them on a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google, and you control it in your phone's backup settings. Generated files (PDFs, photos) are not included.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data stored by the app on your phone.

4. Google components used by the app

The scanner and text recognition features use Google ML Kit and Google Play services. The recognition itself runs on your device. However:

  • The scanner component may be downloaded once through Google Play services, which needs an internet connection the first time.
  • Google may collect limited diagnostic and performance information, and an app-instance identifier, when these components run, as described in Google's own disclosures. We do not receive this information.

Please read Google's documents for details: ML Kit data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions

The app uses the Internet and network-state permissions only so that the Google components above can download the scanner and report diagnostics. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone or all files.

6. Advertising and purchases

This version of PakkaKit shows no ads and has no in-app purchases. If we add ads or a paid subscription later, we will update this policy and the app's Data safety information on Google Play before release, and explain what is collected and by whom.

7. Children

PakkaKit is meant for adults, mainly engineers, contractors and office staff. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Your choices and deleting data

  • Delete saved files from the Files tab, or remove saved templates and sheets inside each tool.
  • Clear the app's storage or uninstall the app in Android settings to remove everything it stores on your phone.
  • Manage Google's diagnostics and backup through your Android and Google account settings.

9. Security

Because your files stay on your phone, their safety depends largely on your phone's own protection (screen lock, updates). Be careful what you share and with whom.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above. The latest version is always at this address.

11. Contact
